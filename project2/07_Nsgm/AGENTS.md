## Manuscript Scope and Conventions

- Do not identify fits as correlated or uncorrelated anywhere in the manuscript, captions, or publication plot labels. Retain the actual covariance choices in the analysis code and provenance. This does not remove physical discussions of data correlations or correlated differences.

- Title: “Excited-State Effects in Nucleon Sigma Terms from a Variational Analysis.” B64 is the main-text focus; A24 and A48 are discussed in Appendix A. Call B64 and A48 physical-point ensembles while retaining their simulated pion masses and the stated isoQCD corrections.
- Use `N\sigma` for the interpolator basis and expectation-value notation for correlators. Do not use internal strings such as `NsgmJNsgm` in prose. For the additional matrix element use `(j,k)=(N\sigma,N\sigma)` after defining the matrix.
- Use `\mathcal J` for interpolators and `\mathcal O` for the scalar insertion. Use `m_\ell` for the degenerate light mass and `m_s,m_c` for strange/charm. Identify lattice quark masses as bare and use `\rm sim`/`\rm iso` superscripts.
- Call both `R_{\rm GEVP}^{d}` and `R_{\rm GEVP}` GEVP-improved ratios. Use “full” for `R_{\rm GEVP}` when useful. Avoid “reduced” in manuscript prose/captions. Do not use “projection” or “projected” for the GEVP operation or its results, including TeX labels and figure legends. Use “GEVP improvement” or “GEVP-improved” instead. This is not a blanket word ban: preserve unrelated physical terminology such as spatial momentum projection and spatially projected measurements. Only the W-weighted ratio has superscript `d`; prime variants are `R_{\rm GEVP}^{\prime}` and `R_{\rm GEVP}^{\prime\prime}`.
- All two-point energies/gaps after GEVP improvement carry tildes. Standard nucleon inputs remain untilded. Use `C_{\rm GEVP}^{\rm 2pt}`, `[v^{-1}]_{N0}`, `t_{\rm ins}^{\rm cut}`, and `\delta/a` for the GEVP-improved denominator, inverse component, insertion cut, and displacement.
- In the light channel, `R_{\rm LG}` is the Laplace-filtered W construction; the strange diagnostic uses the same notation for the filtered full `R_{\rm GEVP}`. Preserve the channel-specific input and the existing legends rather than replacing the strange input with `R_{\rm GEVP}^{d}`. Use `\Delta E_1^{\rm 3pt}` and `\Delta E_1^{\rm 3pt,Lap}` for the standard and Laplace gaps. A prime does not denote Laplace filtering.
- Keep physical uses of "transition", but identify ambiguous references with the existing quantities: matrix elements `\braket{0|\mathcal O|n}`, amplitude `r_{01}`, or term `r_{01}F(\Delta E)`, as appropriate. Use the defined energy-gap symbols instead of shorthand such as "transition gap" or "leading effective transition". Preserve archived prose and coauthor comments.
- Use zero momentum; do not add “spatial” unnecessarily. Finite-volume noninteracting levels are the explicit nonzero-momentum exception.
